Community over Code Glasgow 2026 Speaker Kit

Community over Code Glasgow 2026 (October 11–14) is over. Thank you to everyone who spoke. This page is kept as a record of the speaker kit; the registration, confirmation, and upload deadlines below have passed.

Overview

Thank you for being a speaker at Community over Code Glasgow, taking place October 11-14, 2026, at the Hilton Glasgow, Glasgow, Scotland. Community over Code is the official conference of the Apache Software Foundation, where committers, contributors, and community members behind the world’s most widely deployed open source software gather to share, learn, and shape what comes next.

This was the official event speaker guide. Questions can still go to planners@apachecon.com.

Venue & Travel

Community over Code Glasgow was held at the Hilton Glasgow.

Address: Hilton Glasgow, 1 William St, Glasgow G3 8HT, United Kingdom

The closest airport is Glasgow International Airport (GLA), approximately 15 minutes from the venue. Bus, subway, and train options are available across Glasgow.

Parking: Self-parking £16.50 per day on-site (£3.30/hour, max vehicle height 1.8m). EV charging, secured and covered parking, and in/out privileges available. Valet parking is not available.

Schedule + Confirming Your Bio/Session

The Schedule is on Cvent as a record of the program.

The main conference ran Sunday, October 11 through Wednesday, October 14, with co-located events (Lakehouse Day, Airflow Contributor Days) and workshops on Saturday, October 10 — 160+ sessions across 18 tracks, plus evening Birds of a Feather (BoF) sessions and social events.

Speaker Cancellation and Session Update Policy

This policy applied before the event (the deadline was October 1, 2026) and is kept here for reference.

  • If you were on a solo session and could no longer attend, your session might be canceled and replaced with one from the waitlist.
  • Adding a co-speaker, or replacing a speaker on a multi-speaker session, required track-chair approval.

AV Details + Requirements

Each session room had a screen, projector, and microphones. Speakers supplied their own laptop and adaptors.

Slides
