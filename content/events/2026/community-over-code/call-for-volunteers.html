Call For Volunteers, Community over Code 2026

Community over Code is an event run by the community, for the community. That’s you!

Community over Code Glasgow 2026 (October 11–14) is now over. Thank you to everyone who pitched in before and during the event. The pre-event and on-site volunteer work is done — what’s left is post-event follow-up, and there’s still plenty you can help with.

Share Your Photos

Were you in Glasgow? Add your photos to the shared community album so we can use them in recaps and next year’s promotion:

  • Submit your photos to photos.apachecon.com - Help build the shared event album.

Post-Event Follow-Up Tasks

These tasks still need volunteer owners — thank-yous, recap posts, feedback surveys, collecting slides, success stories, and a lessons-learned writeup:

  • Volunteer Follow-Up Tasks - Speaker thank-yous, feedback, testimonials, lessons-learned
  • Hackathon Follow-Up Tasks - Thank-yous, recap, success stories, feedback survey for the hackathon

Help Improve Our Glasgow Guides

Know Glasgow? These guides live on for future visitors — help us keep them good:

  • Vegetarian & Vegan Restaurants - Add your favourite spots
  • Bars & Pubs - Recommend great places to socialize
  • Tourist Highlights - Add hidden gems and local tips

How to Get Involved

  • Join #community-over-code on the-asf.slack.com
  • Join apachecon.slack.com for day-to-day event coordination
  • Email planners@apachecon.com indicating your interest, and what you’d like to work on.

There are no specific requirements or qualifications for these jobs - just a passion for ASF community and a willingness to collaborate. We’re looking forward to working with you!
