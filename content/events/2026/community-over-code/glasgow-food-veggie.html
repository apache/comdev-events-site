Glasgow Vegetarian & Vegan Restaurants

Community over Code Glasgow 2026 - October 11-14, Hilton Glasgow

A guide to vegetarian and vegan-friendly dining near the Community over Code Glasgow venue (Hilton Glasgow, 1 William St, city centre).

Glasgow was named Scotland’s most veg-friendly city, and you’ll find excellent plant-based options all within walking distance of the venue.

Fully Vegan Restaurants

The Glasvegan

  • What: Plant-based fast food - burgers, hot dogs, loaded fries
  • Where: 50 St Enoch Square, Glasgow G1 4DH (map)
  • Good for: Quick, filling, affordable

Mono

Rose & Grants

  • What: Fully vegan cafe, open 7 days for breakfast and lunch
  • Where: 27 Trongate, Glasgow G1 5EZ (map)
  • Good for: Breakfast/brunch. Try the vegan square sausage roll (a Scottish classic!)

Vegetarian-Friendly Restaurants (with extensive vegan options)

Chaophraya

The Alchemist

Ranjit’s Kitchen

  • What: Homemade Panjabi cuisine, very vegetarian-friendly
  • Where: 607 Pollokshaws Road, Glasgow G41 2QG (map)
  • Good for: Authentic, comforting Indian food at great prices. Closed Mondays.

The Prancing Stag

  • What: Award-winning neighbourhood restaurant (AA Rosette), seasonal Scottish food
  • Where: 1A Ashwood Gardens, Glasgow G13 1NX (map)
  • Good for: Special dinner with excellent veggie options (beside Jordanhill station)

Quick Tips

  • Many Glasgow restaurants label vegan options clearly on menus
  • “VG” = vegan, “V” = vegetarian on most Glasgow menus
  • Deliveroo and Just Eat both have strong vegan filters for Glasgow
  • The West End (Byres Road/Great Western Road area) has the highest concentration of veggie restaurants, accessible via Subway
