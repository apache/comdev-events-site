The Apache Software Foundation’s official conference is underway in Glasgow, Scotland. Four days of talks, workshops, and a hackathon running all week across 20 tracks:

  • AI Plumbing
  • Data Infrastructure & Engineering
  • Streaming (Kafka, Flink, and friends)
  • Search (Lucene, Solr)
  • Cloud Infrastructure (CloudStack)
  • Industrial IoT
  • Community & Governance
  • …and 13 more

This is BY the community, FOR the community. It’s where contributors meet face-to-face, new collaborations start, and the next generation of committers gets onboarded.
