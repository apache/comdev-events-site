#CommunityOverCode #ASF2026Glasgow #OpenSource #ApacheSoftwareFoundation">Community over Code Glasgow 2026 is a wrap!

The Apache Software Foundation’s official conference brought the community together in Glasgow, Scotland for four days of talks, workshops, and a hackathon that ran all week across 20 tracks:

  • AI Plumbing
  • Data Infrastructure & Engineering
  • Streaming (Kafka, Flink, and friends)
  • Search (Lucene, Solr)
  • Cloud Infrastructure (CloudStack)
  • Industrial IoT
  • Community & Governance
  • …and 13 more

This is BY the community, FOR the community. It’s where contributors met face-to-face, new collaborations started, and the next generation of committers got onboarded.
