function copyPost(btn) {
  var post = btn.parentElement;
  var text = post.getAttribute('data-text');

# Share Your Experience!

**Community over Code Glasgow 2026 is a wrap — thank you for being part
of it!** The best promotion for next year's event is you telling people
what this one meant to you. You don't need to be a "social media person."
Post about a talk that stuck with you, a conversation that sparked
something, what you got done in the hackathon room, or a new person you
met. Every post helps the next contributor decide to show up.

Copy, paste, tweak, post. That's it.

**📸 Share your photos:** Please upload your event and hackathon photos to
**[photos.apachecon.com](https://photos.apachecon.com)** — it's the shared
community album we draw on for recaps and next year's promotion. Then drop
a few into your posts.

**Hashtags:** #CommunityOverCode #ASF2026Glasgow #OpenSource

## Tips

- **Personalize it!** These are starting points. Add your own voice,
  mention specific talks you're excited about, tag people.
- **Every post MUST include a link.** Use one of:
  - Conference website: https://communityovercode.apache.org/events/glasgow-2026/
  - Photo album: https://photos.apachecon.com
- **Tag the ASF:** @TheASF on Twitter/X, @TheASF@fosstodon.org on
  Mastodon, Apache Software Foundation on LinkedIn
- **Use the hashtags:** #CommunityOverCode #ASF2026Glasgow
- **Share in your communities:** Project Slack channels, Discord
  servers, mailing lists, meetup groups, Reddit communities
- **Visual content:** If you make a graphic or short video, share it!
  The more formats, the better.
