> **[Community over Code Glasgow 2026](https://communityovercode.apache.org/events/glasgow-2026/)** - October 11-14, Hilton Glasgow

A guide to restaurants near the Community over Code Glasgow venue
(Hilton Glasgow, 1 William St, city centre). The hotel sits right on
the edge of Finnieston — Glasgow's best dining strip — so you're
spoiled for choice within a 5–15 minute walk.

For vegetarian and vegan options specifically, see our dedicated
[Vegetarian & Vegan guide](glasgow-food-veggie.html).

## Fine Dining / Special Occasion

### Cail Bruich ⭐
- **What:** Michelin-starred modern Scottish. Seasonal, foraged, local. Chef Lorna McNee (Great British Menu judge).
- **Where:** 725 Great Western Road, Glasgow G12 8QX ([map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=725%20Great%20Western%20Road%2C%20Glasgow%20G12%208QX))
- **Good for:** The big team dinner. Book well in advance.

### Unalome by Graeme Cheevers ⭐
- **What:** Michelin-starred, elegant modern European in a period Finnieston building.
- **Where:** 36 Kelvingrove Street, Glasgow G3 7RZ ([map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=36%20Kelvingrove%20Street%2C%20Glasgow%20G3%207RZ))
- **Good for:** Special dinner, tasting menus. Very close to venue.

## Scottish / Seafood

### The Finnieston Bar & Restaurant
- **What:** Award-winning seasonal Scottish seafood and craft cocktails. In a beautifully restored 1800s building.
- **Where:** 1125 Argyle Street, Glasgow G3 8ND ([map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=1125%20Argyle%20Street%2C%20Glasgow%20G3%208ND))
- **Good for:** Fresh, sustainable seafood. Excellent gin selection.

### Crabshakk
- **What:** Small, beloved seafood spot — oysters, langoustines, whole crab, chowder.
- **Where:** 1114 Argyle Street, Glasgow G3 8TD ([map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=1114%20Argyle%20Street%2C%20Glasgow%20G3%208TD))
- **Good for:** Informal seafood. No reservations for walk-ins — arrive early or queue.

### Stravaigin
- **What:** "Culinary adventure" — global flavours with local Scottish ingredients.
- **Where:** 28 Gibson Street, Glasgow G12 8NX ([map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=28%20Gibson%20Street%2C%20Glasgow%20G12%208NX))
- **Good for:** Inventive cooking, good veggie options, haggis done right.

## Casual / Small Plates

### Ox and Finch
- **What:** Creative small plates, buzzy atmosphere. One of Glasgow's best-loved restaurants for over a decade.
- **Where:** 920 Sauchiehall Street, Glasgow G3 7TF ([map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=920%20Sauchiehall%20Street%2C%20Glasgow%20G3%207TF))
- **Good for:** Group dinners (sharing plates work well for big tables), cocktails.

### Porter & Rye
- **What:** Steak and cocktails. Dry-aged Scottish beef, craft drinks.
- **Where:** 1131 Argyle Street, Glasgow G3 8ND ([map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=1131%20Argyle%20Street%2C%20Glasgow%20G3%208ND))
- **Good for:** Meat lovers. The rib-eye is the move.

## International

### Cubatas
- **What:** Valencian paella and classic tapas, run by Spanish chefs.
- **Where:** 44 Queen Street, Glasgow G1 3DS ([map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=44%20Queen%20Street%2C%20Glasgow%20G1%203DS))
- **Good for:** Sharing plates, group-friendly, relaxed atmosphere.

### Non Viet
- **What:** Fresh Vietnamese — pho, spring rolls, curries, noodles. One of Glasgow's first Vietnamese restaurants.
- **Where:** 534 Sauchiehall Street, Glasgow G2 3LX ([map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=534%20Sauchiehall%20Street%2C%20Glasgow%20G2%203LX))
- **Good for:** Quick, vibrant, affordable. Also has a vegan offshoot in Partick.

### Gloriosa
- **What:** Italian — inventive pizzas and sharing plates. Focaccia sandwiches at lunch.
- **Where:** 1321 Argyle Street, Glasgow G3 8AB ([map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=1321%20Argyle%20Street%2C%20Glasgow%20G3%208AB))
- **Good for:** Casual Italian, good for groups.

### Chaophraya
- **What:** Thai — largest Thai restaurant in Europe. Dedicated plant-based menu.
- **Where:** 2 Nelson Mandela Place, Glasgow G1 2LL ([map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=2%20Nelson%20Mandela%20Place%2C%20Glasgow%20G1%202LL))
- **Good for:** Big groups, great value set menus, strong veggie/vegan options.

### Topolabamba
- **What:** Mexican - small or large plates in a great atmosphere
- **Where:** 89 St Vincent St, Glasgow G2 5TF ([map](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Topolabamba/@55.861268,-4.2561403,17z/data=!3m1!4b1!4m6!3m5!1s0x4888469e3130b041:0xea280d4a5d4381b5!8m2!3d55.861268!4d-4.2561403!16s%2Fg%2F11b5pjjlt_?hl=en&entry=ttu&g_ep=EgoyMDI2MDgxOS4wIKXMDSoASAFQAw%3D%3D))
- **Good for:** Variety, sharing, good for groups

## Quick Tips

- **Finnieston** (Argyle Street between Kelvinhaugh St and Elderslie
  St) is Glasgow's restaurant row — 5 min walk from the Hilton
- **Merchant City** (east of George Square) is another cluster — 15
  min walk or quick taxi
- **Booking:** Popular spots fill up. Book ahead for groups of 6+.
- **Delivery:** Deliveroo and Just Eat both cover the venue area well
- **Scottish things to try:** Cullen skink (smoked haddock soup),
  haggis/neeps/tatties, Irn-Bru, tablet (Scottish fudge)
